## Full Description (Max 4000 characters)
**SR Foods** is the all-in-one smart manufacturing, inventory, and wholesale distribution ERP designed specifically for food production businesses (Ready-to-eat & ready-to-cook Parota, Chapathi, Pulka, and Puri).

Streamline your factory output, empower your delivery routes, protect your profit margins, and grow wholesale retail orders with zero operational complexity!

### 🌟 Key Features for Food Manufacturers & Distributors:

#### 1. 🫓 Smart Production & Batch Planning
- Record daily production batches with auto-generated lot and batch numbers.
- Automated Bill of Materials (BOM) calculates exact flour (Maida/Atta), refined cooking oil, salt, and food-grade packaging bags required.
- First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO) shelf-life tracking prevents perishable food spoilage.

#### 2. 📲 100% Free WhatsApp Ordering & Digital Billing
- Generate GST-compliant invoices and send them directly to retailers via WhatsApp in 1 tap.
- Send automated, polite Khata balance & payment reminders.
- Share instant digital product price lists and catalogs with Kirana stores, bakeries, supermarkets, and restaurants.
- Public Digital Catalog link (`/catalog`) enables retail shops to place orders directly without installing the app!

#### 3. 🤖 Algorithmic AI Business Advisor (No Paid APIs)
- **Demand Forecasting**: Rolling 30-day exponential sales velocity suggests daily batch sizes to avoid stockouts.
- **Retailer Replenishment Radar**: Tracks individual store order frequency and alerts you the exact day a shop is due for restock.
- **Credit Risk Shield**: 100-point creditworthiness rating alerts you before issuing goods to overdue accounts.

#### 4. 💰 Financial Khata & Ledger Management
- Track customer balances, credit limits, and partial payments (Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer).
- Generate clean PDF bills and 58mm/80mm thermal receipts for van delivery drivers.
- Complete operational expense tracking (Flour, Oil, Gas, Wages, Fuel, Maintenance).

#### 5. 🔒 100% Offline-First & Private
- Works completely without an active internet connection.
- All customer information and turnover records are stored locally in secure on-device SQLite databases.
- Encrypted backup and restore (.srbackup) keeps your business records safe.
