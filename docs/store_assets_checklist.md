# Google Play Store Graphics & Asset Checklist

### 1. App Icon
- [ ] Format: PNG (32-bit), no alpha/transparency.
- [ ] Dimensions: 512px by 512px.
- [ ] Max file size: 1024 KB.
- [ ] Asset reference: `assets/icons/app_icon_512.png`.

### 2. Feature Graphic
- [ ] Format: JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha).
- [ ] Dimensions: 1024px width by 500px height.
- [ ] Recommendation: Bold SR Foods branding with photo of fresh parotas/chapathis and "Smart Food ERP" headline.

### 3. Phone Screenshots (Minimum 4, Recommended 8)
- [ ] Format: JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha).
- [ ] Aspect ratio: 16:9 or 9:16 (minimum dimension 1080px).
- [ ] Screenshot 1: **Manager Executive Dashboard** (Today's revenue, active production goal, low-stock radar).
- [ ] Screenshot 2: **AI Production & Demand Planner** (Recommended batch count & raw materials recipe breakdown).
- [ ] Screenshot 3: **Customer Replenishment Radar** (1-tap WhatsApp order nudge).
- [ ] Screenshot 4: **Fast Wholesale Order Creation** (Multi-item picker, instant total).
- [ ] Screenshot 5: **Professional GST Invoice & WhatsApp Sharing** (Itemized invoice with balance).
- [ ] Screenshot 6: **Retailer Credit Health Shield** (Tier A/B/C creditworthiness score).
- [ ] Screenshot 7: **Public Web Catalog / Digital Dukaan** (Stateless customer ordering link).
- [ ] Screenshot 8: **Stock & Inventory Control** (FEFO batch numbers and runout horizon).

### 4. 7-inch & 10-inch Tablet Screenshots
- [ ] Minimum 1 screenshot for 7-inch tablet (1200 x 1920 or equivalent).
- [ ] Minimum 1 screenshot for 10-inch tablet (1600 x 2560 or equivalent).
