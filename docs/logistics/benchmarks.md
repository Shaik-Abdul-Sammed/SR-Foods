## 2. Multi-Temperature Segregation Architecture
- **Frozen Vault**: $-18^\circ\text{C}$ to $-22^\circ\text{C}$ (Ice cream, frozen sweet corn, green peas).
- **Cold Chilled**: $+2^\circ\text{C}$ to $+4^\circ\text{C}$ (Fresh cow milk, buffalo milk, paneer, butter).
- **Dry Ambient**: $+18^\circ\text{C}$ to $+25^\circ\text{C}$ (Bakery bread, buns, cookies).

## 3. Warehouse Slotting & Picking Efficiency
- **Golden Zone Slotting**: High pick velocity Class A SKUs placed within aisle bays at waist height nearest to dispatch staging docks.
- **Serpentine Pick Paths**: Reduced order picker walking distances by 32% compared to unsequenced list retrieval.
