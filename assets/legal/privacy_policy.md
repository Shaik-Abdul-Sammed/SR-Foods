# Privacy Policy for SR Foods

**Last Updated:** November 2, 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 2, 2026  

SR Foods ("we", "our", or "the App") operates as a smart business enterprise resource planning (ERP) and distribution platform for ready-made food products (Parota, Chapathi, Pulka, Puri). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal and business data handled by our application.

---

## 1. Zero Cloud Upload of Customer Data
All operational data, including retailer names, shop addresses, orders, invoices, production batch logs, and ledger account balances, are stored **strictly locally on your device** in a private SQLite database (`sr_foods.db`). We do not maintain remote cloud servers that copy or collect your business data.

---

## 2. Permissions & Data Handling

### A. Contacts Permission (`READ_CONTACTS`)
- **Purpose**: Used solely to allow you to optionally select a customer or shop owner from your phone address book when creating or editing a customer record, eliminating manual typing.
- **Processing**: Contact details are processed **100% on-device** and are never shared, uploaded to third parties, transmitted over the internet, or used for marketing.
- **Control**: You can grant or revoke contacts permission at any time in your Android system settings without losing access to other features of SR Foods.

### B. Photos & Media (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / Photo Picker)
- **Purpose**: Used only when you choose to attach product catalog photos or photograph physical vendor expense receipts.
- **Processing**: The App access is restricted to the specific media files you explicitly choose.

### C. Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)
- **Purpose**: Displays local on-device alerts for low inventory safety thresholds, batch completion, and database backup reminders. No remote push notifications or tracking tokens are generated.

---

## 3. WhatsApp Integration
SR Foods allows you to share itemized invoices, payment reminders, and product catalogs directly via WhatsApp. This functionality operates using official `wa.me` intent links. The text message content is transferred directly to your local WhatsApp application for your review before you tap send. SR Foods has no access to your WhatsApp account or messages.

---

## 4. No Advertising & No Third-Party Trackers
SR Foods does not integrate commercial ad networks, third-party user tracking SDKs, or data brokers.

---

## 5. Security & Encrypted Backups
Local databases are secured behind system sandboxing. Optional manual backup files (`.srbackup`) created by the manager can be exported and password-protected via AES encryption.
